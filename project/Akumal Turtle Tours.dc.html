Good to know

Do I need to know how to swim?+

Basic comfort helps, but every guest wears a life vest and stays with a guide. Non-swimmers welcome.

Are turtle sightings guaranteed?+

Akumal Bay is a resident feeding ground, so sightings are very common year-round. We never chase or touch the turtles.

What's included in the price?+

Mask, snorkel, fins, life vest, bay conservation fee and your certified guide. Bring reef-safe sunscreen.

How do I get to Akumal?+

Akumal sits on the Cancún–Tulum highway, about 35 minutes south of Cancún airport. We'll send exact meeting-point details after booking.

Ready to dive in?

We confirm your spot within the hour. Tours run daily from 7 AM.
